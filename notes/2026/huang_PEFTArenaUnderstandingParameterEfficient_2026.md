> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the overlooked stability-plasticity trade-off in parameter-efficient finetuning, this work introduces PEFT-Arena to jointly measure task performance and capability retention, revealing that spectral regularity governs stability and that interpolating tuned and base models improves the trade-off.
