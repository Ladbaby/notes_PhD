> **TLDR**:: To address the modality gap and inter-variable dependency challenges in using LLMs for multivariate time series forecasting, this paper proposes Granger-TSllm, which employs a residual-quantized tokenizer for compact embeddings and a Granger causality module to enhance inter-variable correlation modeling.
