> **TLDR**:: To overcome architectural and computational barriers preventing Large Language Models from dynamically adapting during inference, the paper introduces In-Place Test-Time Training, which repurposes final MLP projection matrices as fast weights with a next-token-prediction-aligned objective for efficient, drop-in continual learning.
