> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the prohibitive quadratic computational complexity of existing STGNNs for large-scale traffic forecasting, this paper proposes BigST, a linear complexity model that uses scalable feature extraction and linearized global spatial convolution to efficiently capture long-range spatio-temporal dependencies.
