> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the unreliability of existing federated active learning methods in medical scenarios with domain shifts, this paper proposes Federated Evidential Active Learning that calibrates data evaluation using Dirichlet-based evidential models to capture and balance aleatoric and epistemic uncertainties.
