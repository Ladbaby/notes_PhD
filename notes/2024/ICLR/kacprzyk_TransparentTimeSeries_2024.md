> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the lack of holistic transparency in time series forecasting caused by point-focused bottom-up approaches, this paper proposes a bi-level framework and develops TIMEVIEW, a transparent model with interactive visualization for understanding both high-level trends and low-level properties.
