> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the failure of existing methods to capture fine-grained variable-specific features and correlations in irregularly sampled medical time series, this paper proposes a knowledge-empowered dynamic graph network that leverages textual medical knowledge to model variable-specific temporal dependencies and time-varying inter-variable correlations.
