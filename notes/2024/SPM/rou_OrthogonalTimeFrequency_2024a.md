> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To overcome doubly dispersive channels in high-mobility B5G/6G systems that degrade OFDM performance, this study compares OTFS and AFDM waveforms, demonstrating their superior robustness against intercarrier interference and enhanced integrated sensing capabilities.
