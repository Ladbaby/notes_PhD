> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the difficulty of adaptively balancing local and global temporal features in multivariant time series imputation, this paper proposes Score-CDM, which uses a Score-weighted Convolution Module and an Adaptive Reception Module to dynamically adjust receptive fields via spectral domain convolution.
