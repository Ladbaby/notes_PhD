> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To overcome slow learning in recurrent backpropagation caused by decaying error signals, the authors introduce Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), which uses constant error carousels and multiplicative gates to maintain stable gradient flow across long time intervals.
