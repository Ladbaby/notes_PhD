> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the inability of existing unsupervised domain adaptation methods to handle distinct sensor distributions in multivariate time-series data, the SEA model aligns local and global sensor features while modeling spatial-temporal dependencies via a multi-branch self-attention mechanism.
