> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the challenge of engineering predictive features for noisy multivariate time series, this paper proposes a framework that models series as cumulative sums of trajectory increments governed by a spin-gas dynamical Ising model to enable automated, bias-aware feature generation.
