> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the lack of unified time series foundation models due to data sparsity and modality misalignment, Time-LLM repurposes frozen LLMs for forecasting by reprogramming inputs with text prototypes and using Prompt-as-Prefix to align modalities.
