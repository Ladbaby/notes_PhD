> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address inefficiencies in cross-modality interaction and annotation gaps in vision foundation models, the paper introduces APE, a universal model that unifies detection, segmentation, and grounding through instance-level sentence-object matching and open-vocabulary reformulation.
