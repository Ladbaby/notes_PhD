> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the high memory and computing bottlenecks of Transformers in long-term time series forecasting, this paper proposes TSMixer, a lightweight MLP-based architecture enhanced with online reconciliation heads, hybrid channel modeling, and gated attention to efficiently capture temporal properties.
