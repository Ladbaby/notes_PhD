> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the lack of large-scale public datasets and standardized benchmarks for coronary artery segmentation in CTA images, this paper introduces a comprehensive dataset, implements a comparative benchmark, and proposes a strong baseline method using multi-scale patch fusion and two-stage processing.
