> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To overcome the limitations of existing zero-shot forecasting models that perform inconsistently on data-sparse time series, the authors propose ForecastPFN, a prior-data fitted network trained on synthetic distributions to deliver accurate, fast predictions via single-pass Bayesian inference.
