> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address time series classification with noisy labels where standard small-loss criteria fail due to frequency perturbations, this paper proposes Scale-teaching, a multi-scale framework that uses cross-scale fusion and graph-based label propagation to robustly identify clean samples and correct errors.
