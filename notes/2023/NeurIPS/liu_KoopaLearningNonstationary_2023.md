> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the challenge of non-stationarity in real-world time series, Koopa disentangles time-variant and time-invariant components using Fourier filters and advances their dynamics via context-aware Koopman predictors within a deep residual structure.

> [!cite]
> **Bibliography**:: Liu, Y., Li, C., Wang, J., & Long, M. (2023). Koopa: Learning Non-stationary Time Series Dynamics with Koopman Predictors. _Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems_, _36_, 12271–12290.

> [!info]
> **Contribution**:: 以 Koopman 算子视角解耦时变/非时变分量；理论推导部分较为密集，实验增益的稳定性有待验证
