> **TLDR**:: To unify discriminative contrastive representations with semantic latent structures for clustering, the paper introduces Mixture of Contrastive Experts (MiCE), which uses a gating function to partition data and multiple experts for contrastive discrimination, solved via a scalable EM algorithm.
