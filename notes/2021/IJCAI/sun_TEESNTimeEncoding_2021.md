> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address suboptimal predictions in irregularly sampled time series caused by artificial dependencies and biased learning, this work proposes a Time Encoding mechanism and a novel Time Encoding Echo State Network that embed time information in the complex domain to accurately model irregular intervals and sampling rates.
