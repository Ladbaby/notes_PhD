> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the unexplored impact of reasoning strategies on zero-shot time-series forecasting, the authors propose ReC4TS, a benchmark that identifies self-consistency as the most effective test-time strategy and provides a novel dataset and scaling law for future research.
