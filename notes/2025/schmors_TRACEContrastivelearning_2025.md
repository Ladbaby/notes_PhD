> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the failure of existing contrastive learning methods to exploit multi-trial structures in neural time series, TRACE generates positive pairs by averaging across trial subsets to learn embeddings that capture biologically relevant continuous variation and cell-type clusters.
