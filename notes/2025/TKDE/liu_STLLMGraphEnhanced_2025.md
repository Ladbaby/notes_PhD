> **TLDR**:: To address the poor generalization of existing traffic prediction models across diverse contexts, this paper proposes ST-LLM+, which enhances large language models with a proximity-based adjacency matrix and a partially frozen graph attention module to capture complex spatio-temporal dependencies.
