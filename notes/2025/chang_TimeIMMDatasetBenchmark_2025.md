> **TLDR**:: To bridge the gap between clean benchmarks and messy real-world time series, this work introduces Time-IMM, a dataset capturing nine types of cause-driven irregularity, alongside IMM-TSF, a benchmark library enabling asynchronous multimodal forecasting.
