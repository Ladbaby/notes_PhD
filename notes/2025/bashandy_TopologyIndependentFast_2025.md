> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the need for guaranteed node and adjacency protection in Segment Routing, the paper introduces Topology Independent Loop-Free Alternate Fast Reroute, which extends existing IP FRR concepts to ensure coverage in any two-connected network by aligning FRR paths with expected post-convergence routes.
