> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the neglect of short-term dynamics and variable-specific characteristics in multivariate time series prediction, this paper proposes MR-Transformer, which models temporal and variable resolutions by combining adaptive segment-based short-term pattern extraction with a temporal convolution module for individual variable features.
