> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To address the optimization dilemma where existing time series imputation models overfit local noise and fail to capture global structure under high missing rates, this paper proposes Glocal-IB, a model-agnostic paradigm that introduces a Global Alignment loss to align latent representations and improve generalization.
