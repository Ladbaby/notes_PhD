> [!info]
> **TLDR**:: To overcome the lack of explicit reasoning in fast-thinking time series forecasting, the authors propose Time-R1, a two-stage reinforcement fine-tuning framework that equips LLMs with slow-thinking capabilities via a fine-grained multi-objective reward and GRIP optimization.
