Next, it integrates predictions and confidence scores from plug-in classifiers, e.g., domain-specific time series models, as incontext examples. Finally, ReasonTSC guides the LLM through a structured reasoning process: it evaluates the initial assessment, backtracks to consider alternative hypotheses, and compares their merits before arriving at a final classification.
