> **TLDR**:: To mitigate performance degradation caused by noisy or incomplete hypergraph structures, this paper proposes an end-to-end Hypergraph Structure Learning framework that simultaneously optimizes the network architecture and learns a task-specific, sparse hypergraph via two-stage sampling and contrastive learning.
